# WARP

## Purpose

WARP is the materialization process of Atlas.

Its purpose is to ensure that every stable insight leaves the temporary
workspace and becomes part of Atlas.

A chat may end.

Atlas must not lose knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Beginning of WARP

WARP begins when the current work is considered stable enough to be
preserved.

From this moment onward, no new ideas are developed until
materialization is complete.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Objectives

A successful WARP ensures that:

-   stable knowledge is preserved,
-   canonical documents are updated,
-   no confirmed insight remains only inside the chat,
-   Atlas can continue from its own documentation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Principles

1.  Atlas is the source of truth.
2.  Existing documents are updated before creating new ones.
3.  New documents arise only from genuine need.
4.  Every change must be verifiable.
5.  WARP ends only after verification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Completion Criteria

WARP is complete only if all of the following are true:

-   All confirmed knowledge has been materialized.
-   Canonical documents are synchronized.
-   No confirmed insight exists only in the conversation.
-   The current state is documented.
-   Atlas can continue without this chat.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Final State

When these conditions are fulfilled, Atlas reaches the state:

**Atlas is synchron.**
