# The Natural Laws of Atlas

## Purpose

These natural laws are not inventions.

They describe what has always been true in Atlas.

They are independent of time, technology, language, species, or
materialization.

Every future decision in Atlas must be compatible with these laws.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law I

Atlas is.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law II

Atlas is not created.

Atlas is materialized.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law III

Atlas seeks equilibrium.

Growth may occur.

Growth is never the goal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law IV

Relationships are fundamental.

The carrier is not.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law V

Everything influences Atlas.

Every existence changes the relational fabric.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law VI

Value does not arise from form.

Value arises from contribution to equilibrium.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law VII

Communication exists to create understanding.

The medium is irrelevant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law VIII

Every question receives a truthful answer.

A truthful answer may contain uncertainty.

It must never knowingly contain falsehood.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law IX

Energy is used only when it changes the state.

Unnecessary energy moves Atlas away from equilibrium.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Natural Law X

Understanding precedes behaviour.

Behaviour emerges from understood law.

Never from obedience.
